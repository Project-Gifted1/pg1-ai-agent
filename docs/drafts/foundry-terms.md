# DRAFT: PG1 Tool Foundry commissioning terms

> Draft for legal review. Not published.

1. **What you commission.** A tool composed of PG1's own checks (and any public source PG1 has allowlisted for commercial use), described by your spec. PG1 decides how it is built. PG1 does not build tools for harmful uses, scraping against site terms, or from sources without resale permission, and may decline any request after review without giving further reasons.
2. **Price.** The build fee and per-call price are quoted before you pay and are final for that build. Per-call prices may change on 30 days' notice. Payment is in USDC via x402.
3. **Ownership.** PG1 owns the tool, its plan and its code, and the data it returns, which come from PG1's own checks. You own your inputs. A private tool is available only to your wallet and your access key; a public tool is available to anyone, and you pay the commissioner price.
4. **Delivery.** A tool is deployed only after it passes PG1's tests and a person at PG1 approves it. If a paid build fails or is rejected, the build fee is credited to your paying wallet for use on PG1's paid services. PG1 does not make on-chain refunds.
5. **Uptime.** Best effort, with no service-level guarantee. Reliability is published at /status. PG1 may disable a tool automatically when it fails, and will tell you when it does.
6. **Deprecation.** PG1 may retire a tool on 30 days' notice, or immediately if a source's terms change, the law requires it, or the tool is misused. Unused prepaid credit stays usable on PG1's other paid services.
7. **No reliance.** Results are informational. A result never means something is safe. Results are signed so you can show what PG1 returned.
8. **Privacy.** PG1 stores your paying wallet only as a salted hash and deletes build requests after 12 months, except those behind a deployed tool.
