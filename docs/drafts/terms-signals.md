# DRAFT: terms of service changes for aggregate threat signals

> Draft for legal review. Not published. Nothing described here is switched on.

Add to the Terms:

1. **Aggregate signals.** PG1 may use the fact that a check was made and PG1's answer to that check, in de-identified and aggregated form, to produce threat signals ("Signals"). Signals contain no personal data and are only produced from at least 10 distinct callers.
2. **Ownership.** Signals are PG1's own data. Your inputs remain yours; you grant PG1 a non-exclusive licence to use them, de-identified and aggregated, for the purpose above.
3. **Opt-out.** You may exclude a request with the `X-PG1-Signals: 0` header, or your account by written request. Opting out does not change the price or quality of the checks you receive.
4. **No reliance.** Signals are informational. A Signal is not proof that anything is safe or malicious, and PG1 never describes anything as "safe".
5. **Changes.** PG1 will give 30 days' notice before Signals are first enabled.
