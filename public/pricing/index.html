Pay per check

0.002 USD a check

  • No account: pay as you go once your free checks are used
  • USDC on Base, through the standard x402 payment flow

Daily allowances reset at 00:00 UTC; monthly quotas on the 1st of the month at 00:00 UTC. A licence over its monthly quota gets a clear error naming the quota and its reset date: results are never cut short or quietly degraded.

What counts as a check

  • One completed call to check_agent_message or check_repo_trap, over MCP (/api/mcp), A2A (/api/a2a) or REST (/api/repo-trap).
  • A call rejected for bad input (too large, malformed) is not counted.
  • A check that names domains or wallets runs PG1’s own lookups for them inside the same check; those are not counted separately.

Keys

Send your key in the X-API-KEY header. The same header carries a free key, a Builder or Team licence key from your card payment receipt, or a Team key. Over the limit without a licence, the response is an x402 payment challenge for 0.002 USD, with links to /keys and this page.

Team keys: with your Team licence key in X-API-KEY, POST /api/keys/team creates a team key (up to 4, so 5 keys with the licence key), GET lists them and DELETE ?id= revokes one.

What a result means

A clean result is no_findings: PG1’s rules found nothing. It is never “safe”, and it does not prove the content or project is harmless.
